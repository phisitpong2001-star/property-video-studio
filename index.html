How it works

  1. Add the listingEnter the details and photos, or let the form fill itself from what you paste.
  2. ChoosePick the narration style, song, caption style and post formats.
  3. Review the planSee every scene's picture and script before anything is made.
  4. Approve and makeThe studio edits the pictures, adds the voice and captions, and renders the result.
