Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026

This policy explains what Property Video Studio (the "service") handles and why. The service is operated by Voice Intelligent Systems Co. Ltd ("we"), who you can reach at phisit.t@mentrarecording.com.

1. What we handle

  • Listing details: property information, price, location, and any owner or contact details you enter.
  • Listing files: photos and floor plans you upload, and anything you attach to fill in the form automatically.
  • Voice: if you choose to create your own narration voice, your recording, the provider's voice ID, and your name and the time you gave consent.
  • What the service makes: scripts, captions, edited pictures, videos and photo posts.
  • Posting records: group or channel names, post dates and links to posts, and an activity log of actions in the service.

2. Why we use it

We use this information to plan and make the content you ask for, show it to you for review, and keep a record of where it was posted. The service has no advertising or data-sale feature.

4. TikTok

Connecting TikTok is in development and isn't available yet, so the service doesn't currently receive any TikTok account data. When it becomes available, connecting will be your choice and will work like this:

  • Your basic profile (permission user.info.basic): your TikTok ID, display name and profile picture, so you can see which account is connected.
  • Sending videos (permission video.upload): the video you choose is sent to your TikTok drafts, where you finish and post it yourself.
  • Access tokens: the service stores the tokens TikTok issues, encrypted on the server, so it can act on your request.

You'll be able to disconnect by removing the app's access in your TikTok settings. We'll update this section if any of this changes before the feature launches.

5. Facebook and Instagram

Connecting Facebook and Instagram is in development and limited to testing by the operator. When you connect, you choose which assets to share. The service then handles:

  • Account details: the ID and username of the Instagram professional account, the ID and name of any Facebook Page, and your business portfolio ID.
  • Access tokens: stored encrypted on the server and never sent to your browser.
  • Publishing: the video and caption you confirm in the preview are sent to the account you chose, and published publicly as a Reel. The service keeps a record of the post and its link.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data until it's deleted. There's currently no automatic deletion period.

  • Your voice: deleting your voice in the service removes it from ElevenLabs and deletes your recording. Videos already made stay as they are.
  • Listings: deleting a listing removes its record. Files made from it, such as videos, may remain until you ask us to delete them.

7. Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data by emailing phisit.t@mentrarecording.com. You may have further rights under the law where you live. Our Data Deletion page explains how to disconnect an account and remove its data.

8. This website

This website sets no cookies and has no analytics. It loads its font from Google Fonts, so Google receives your browser's request, including your IP address. GitHub Pages, which hosts this website, may keep standard server logs.

9. Children

The service isn't meant for anyone under 18.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the effective date.
