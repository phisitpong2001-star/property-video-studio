Data Deletion

Effective 2 November 2026

This page explains how to remove your data from Property Video Studio, operated by Voice Intelligent Systems Co. Ltd. Connecting social accounts is in development and is limited to testing by the operator.

1. Ask us to delete your data

Email phisit.t@mentrarecording.com and tell us the Facebook Page, Instagram account or TikTok account involved. We'll confirm by email when it's done. Requests aren't automated, and we don't currently offer an automatic deletion callback.

2. Disconnect an account in the app

On the Social media page, choose Disconnect. This deletes the stored access tokens and account details for that connection from the app, and asks the platform to end the app's access.

Disconnecting keeps the app's record of past posts, including the caption, the account name and the link to each post. Email us to have those records deleted too.

3. Remove the app's access on the platform

  • Facebook and Instagram: remove Property Video Studio from your business portfolio's connected integrations, or from your Facebook or Instagram account's apps and websites settings.
  • TikTok: remove the app from the apps with access in your TikTok settings.

4. What deletion doesn't cover

  • Posts already published: these stay on the platform until you delete them there. TikTok drafts stay in your TikTok inbox until you finish or delete them.
  • Copies held by service providers: each provider listed in our Privacy Policy keeps data under its own rules.
